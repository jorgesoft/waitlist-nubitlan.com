# Growth to-do: local credibility and backlinks

Off-site work that helps nubitlan.com rank and earns trust from Salvadoran
businesses. None of it needs code. Tick items off as they are done.

## Search engines (do first, about 30 minutes)

- [ ] Verify nubitlan.com in **Google Search Console** (DNS TXT record via Cloudflare) and submit `https://nubitlan.com/sitemap.xml`
- [ ] Verify in **Bing Webmaster Tools** (it can import from Search Console) and submit the sitemap
- [ ] After the first deploy, test a blog post and `/correo` with the [Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) and share a link in WhatsApp/LinkedIn to confirm the preview card

## Local presence

- [ ] Create a **Google Business Profile** (service-area business, El Salvador; category "Consultor de seguridad informática"); link to the site and post monthly
- [ ] Join or list with **CAMARASAL** (Cámara de Comercio e Industria de El Salvador)
- [ ] Get listed with **AMPES** (Asociación de Medianas y Pequeñas Empresas Salvadoreñas)
- [ ] Look at other directories: ASI, CONAMYPE's provider network, local tech communities (e.g. meetups and university IT clubs)

## Content and backlinks

- [ ] Pitch 2–3 **guest articles** on the Data Protection Law to local business media and chamber newsletters, each linking to the related blog guide
- [ ] Offer **talks or webinars** (chambers, universities, coworkings); ask for a link from the event page
- [ ] **Republish each blog post on LinkedIn** (company page + founders) a few days after it goes live, with a link back to the original
- [ ] Share the **email security checker** (`nubitlan.com/correo`) in LinkedIn posts. Example: "We checked 50 Salvadoran companies' domains; X% have no DMARC", based on public data only and with no company named without consent

## Social proof

- [ ] Turn the case study into a **named testimonial** (logo + quote) if the client agrees
- [ ] Ask happy clients for a **Google review** on the Business Profile
